Deleting your Ödev Odak account

You can delete your Ödev Odak account from within the app.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Ödev Odak.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Open Delete account.
  4. Complete the final confirmation.

What is removed?

To the extent technically possible, deletion removes your Ödev Odak account, its cloud backup, parent/family connections and the app data for this account on the device.

If Premium is linked to this account, it ends with the account and cannot be moved to another account. Deleting the account does not cancel an active subscription; cancel it separately in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. For a refund of a payment made through Apple, use Apple’s “Report a Problem” page (reportaproblem.apple.com).

Source assignments in Google Classroom and data in your Google account are not deleted by Ödev Odak. Manage them separately through Google’s own services.

If you can’t open the app

For help deleting your account or data, write to destek@odevodak.com. We may ask for additional information to confirm that you own the account.

Some deleted records may not disappear immediately from backups, technical logs or legal retention systems; they are kept only as long as necessary and then deleted or anonymized.
