Work turned in on Classroom may be shown as turned in in the App; it is shown separately if that record was not verified with photos. The photo check in the App does not upload files to Classroom or turn anything in there; if submission is required, complete it in Google Classroom.

3. Homework details and photos

You are responsible for checking the assignment name, subject, content and due date before saving and correcting them where needed. Text read from a photo may be a draft with errors, gaps or days matched incorrectly. On the add-from-photo screen you can edit each day and assignment individually, remove ones you don’t want and set separate due dates.

Before starting the photo check, make sure the photos belong to you or that you are entitled to use them. Do not include faces, IDs, student numbers, addresses, other students’ details or sensitive personal information in photos.

4. Gemini photo check and 18+ declaration

The photo check may send up to four sample photos you submit in the App, together with the related homework metadata, to an automatic AI service. The result may be completed, incomplete/unclear or a connection/error result. Results are for assistance only; they are not a teacher’s assessment, an official submission or proof of academic accuracy.

Age and authorization declaration for Gemini: In accordance with the Gemini API Additional Terms in force, before starting the photo check you must declare and confirm that you are 18 or older and authorized to use this feature. This does not mean the App verifies your age from your face or ID with Gemini; the App does not produce a biometric age verification result. If you are under 18, or unsure whether you meet the requirement, do not use the photo check.

The current Gemini API terms require API users to be 18 or older and prohibit using the API as part of an application directed at, or likely to be accessed by, people under 18. In a distribution with student or child users, this feature should not be enabled until compatibility with the provider’s terms has been confirmed. Adding this declaration to the Terms does not change the provider’s rule. Current text: Gemini API Additional Terms.

The photo check also requires you to give explicit consent to sending in the App. Without consent, no photo is sent. You can review a false positive or false negative and try again with clearer photos.

5. Google Classroom connection

You start the Classroom connection and approve the requested Google permissions on Google’s screen. The App aims to read only the permitted course and student assignment data. A Google administrator or school policy may restrict third-party access; in that case the connection may not work.

Disconnecting Classroom does not automatically delete previously imported assignments from the device. While the App is open, it syncs roughly every minute and when it returns to the foreground; there is no continuous background sync while the App is fully closed. Official submissions, grades and deletions in Google Classroom are managed in Google’s own service.

6. Cloud backup and data deletion

Signed-in users’ homework records may be backed up to the main Ödev Odak account. The Classroom account is not the backup account. Backups may be delayed by internet outages, service outages or device problems; do not rely on the Service as the only copy of important school records.

You can delete your account in the App under Settings → Delete account. After the final confirmation, to the extent technically possible, your account, your Ödev Odak cloud backup, your parent connections and the app data for this account on the device are deleted. Source assignments in Google Classroom and Google account data are not deleted by Ödev Odak; you must manage them separately in Google Classroom. Account deletion cannot be undone.

7. Parent connection and focus mode

Parental controls are optional. The parent dashboard does not open unless the student and parent accounts approve the connection in separate steps. While the connection is active, the parent can see the homework and submission statuses, photo verification status and limited device focus status shown by the App; they cannot access photos, Classroom tokens or the contents of the chosen apps.

Edits or focus commands sent by the parent may sync to the student’s device. If the “Personal focus permission” switch on the student’s device is off, the parent’s focus rule is not applied. Permission and app choices are managed separately by Apple on each device.
